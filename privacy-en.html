Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 October 2026 · HelloStoryboard (provided by Studioroom) · 한국어

HelloStoryboard has no accounts and collects no personal data. The app does not connect to the internet. Your boards, imported images and recordings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

1. Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store or use any personal data — no name, email, phone number, location or device identifiers. There is no sign-in, and no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The app contains no code that sends data over the internet.

2. Device permissions

PermissionWhenHandling
MicrophoneAsked only the first time you tap record (sound or voice note)Recordings are saved only in the project folder on your device. Everything except recording works if you decline.
Photos / FilesWhen you pick a reference image, video or PDFOnly the files you pick are read into the project. The app does not access your whole photo library.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Projects — drawings, shot data, imported images, sound and video, and recordings, in the app's documents folder (Files app on iPad, app-specific storage on Android).
  • Settings — UI language, tool and brush settings, workspace layout.
  • Crash log — a local file (crash.log) written if the app crashes. It is never sent automatically; if you attach it to a support email we use it only to fix the problem.

Deleting the app deletes this data (files you exported elsewhere remain).

4. Export and sharing

PDF, image and video export only create files where you choose. Sending them to other apps is your choice, and those apps' privacy policies apply.

5. Payment

HelloStoryboard is a paid app sold on the App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google process the payment; we receive no payment information. There are no in-app purchases.

6. Third parties

Since we collect no personal data, we share none with third parties.

7. Children

The app collects no personal data and works the same for users of any age.

8. Changes

If we ever add a feature that sends data off the device (such as cloud sync), we will update this policy before it takes effect and announce it in the app's release notes.
